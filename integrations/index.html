Integrations

Works with the stack
you already bought.

Overtone is not a replacement for your telephony platform, your recorder, or your workforce management suite. It is the analysis layer that sits across all of them — reads what they capture, and writes results back where your teams already work.

60+ certified connectors REST API and webhooks Custom connectors in ~2 weeks
01

Connect

Authenticate once against your platform's API with a service account scoped to read recordings and call metadata. No agent desktop install, no network appliance, no change to how calls are routed or recorded.

02

Sync

Overtone discovers completed calls and pulls audio plus metadata — agent, queue, disposition, duration, and any custom attributes you already tag. Live-stream mode is available where the platform supports it.

03

Push back

Scores, coaching tasks, sentiment, and risk flags flow into your WFM, CRM, and BI stack. Your teams keep working in the tools they know; the intelligence just shows up there.

Build your own

If we don't have a connector, you can write one.

Everything the product does is available over a documented REST API with webhooks for event push. Homegrown recorder, in-house CRM, an internal scheduling system nobody outside your company has heard of — all fine.
