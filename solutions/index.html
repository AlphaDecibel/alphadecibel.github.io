For agents

"Tell me exactly what
to do differently."

Agents don't resist measurement. They resist measurement that feels arbitrary, arrives three weeks late, and comes from a sample they suspect was cherry-picked. Overtone removes all three objections at once.

  • Every call counted — no more suspicion that a bad sample defined the month
  • Same rubric for everyone, applied identically, with the definition visible
  • One clear next drill instead of six vague improvement areas
  • Your own wins surfaced — the save you handled brilliantly gets shared, not just the miss
  • Dispute with evidence — open the dimension and the cited utterance side by side

Contact centers that made scores visible to agents in week one saw adoption run roughly three times higher than those that piloted quietly with supervisors first. Transparency is the change-management strategy.

For supervisors

"Stop making me
hunt for calls."

The single biggest time sink in frontline management is finding something worth coaching on. Overtone hands supervisors a ranked queue every morning with the clip, the timestamp, and the reason already attached.

  • Prioritized coaching queue — who needs attention, on what, and why, in order
  • Team heatmap exposing whether a gap is one agent or a training problem
  • Session prep done — agenda, clips, and peer exemplars assembled in advance
  • Risk alerts in near real time on churn language, escalation, and compliance failure
  • Follow-through visible — proof the last session actually changed behavior

≈ 6 hours per supervisor per week returned to the floor.

For quality & compliance leaders

"Prove it to the regulator."

Sampling was always a defensible compromise, never a good answer. When an examiner asks whether a disclosure was delivered on every eligible call in a fourteen-state footprint, "we reviewed 1.8% and extrapolated" is a difficult sentence to say out loud.

  • 100% coverage — every call, every requirement, every month
  • Calibration control — measure and close analyst-to-analyst drift
  • Version-locked rubrics so a score from March is still reproducible in December
  • Evidence trail per finding — utterance, timestamp, audio, and rubric version
  • Signed exports for audit, in CSV or PDF, on a schedule
  • Regulatory packs for state disclosure, consent, DNC, PCI capture windows, and complaint handling

For executives

"Is any of this
worth the money?"

Quality programs have historically been defended on principle, because nobody could connect a scorecard to a P&L line. Overtone was built to end that argument in whichever direction the data points.

  • Revenue influenced by conversation quality, by segment and by quarter
  • Ranked levers — where the next dollar of coaching capacity returns most
  • Cost per contact and handle-time movement attributable to specific behaviors
  • Site and BPO comparison on a rubric everyone is actually being held to
  • Voice-of-customer themes straight from what people said, not survey response bias
  • Board-ready exports with methodology attached
By industry

Where the stakes are highest.

Overtone shows up most often in industries where a conversation is simultaneously a sales moment, a service moment, and a regulated disclosure.

01

Insurance

Carriers and agencies where a quote call is a compliance event. Track state-specific disclosure delivery and ordering, replacement-policy conversations, needs analysis depth, and the objection patterns that decide whether a quote binds. Our origin story — the first Overtone deployment analyzed auto and home quote calls for a regional carrier.

02

Banking & credit unions

Fraud claim handling, collections tone and FDCPA exposure, hardship conversations, and cross-sell that has to stay on the right side of suitability. Data residency is usually the deciding factor here, which is why the on-premises deployment exists.

03

Healthcare & payers

Member services, prior authorization, benefit explanation, and appointment access. PHI is masked before persistence, and the whole system can run inside your HIPAA boundary with no third-party processor in the path.

04

Telecommunications & utilities

High-volume retention and win-back operations where a two-point movement in save rate is worth more than the entire quality program costs. Churn-language detection routes at-risk conversations to retention while the customer is still on the line.

05

Retail & e-commerce

Seasonal surges with heavy temporary staffing, where new-hire ramp time is the constraint. Score every call from an agent's first day and compress ramp from weeks to days with targeted drills instead of generic training.

06

BPO & outsourced operations

For BPOs, an objective shared rubric turns a contentious QA conversation with the client into a measurable SLA. For brands using BPOs, it means your outsourced sites and your internal sites are finally being graded the same way.
