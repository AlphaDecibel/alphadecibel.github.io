Before you write

Answers to the usual first questions.

Will you sign our NDA before we send audio?

Yes, and we'd expect you to insist. We'll sign yours rather than push ours, and we can usually turn it around in a day or two. Audio sent for evaluation is deleted on request and automatically after 30 days.

Can we do a proof of concept without any deployment?

Yes. The evaluation runs entirely on our side using audio you send us — no connectors, no infrastructure, no change to your environment. If you need even that to happen inside your own network, we can arrange an on-premises evaluation instead; it takes longer to set up but some security teams require it.

What do you need from us to give a real price?

Annual conversation volume, deployment model, how many distinct rubrics you'd run, and which systems we'd connect to. That's enough for a firm number rather than a range. Pricing is on volume analyzed, not per seat.

We're early — is it too soon to talk?

No. A meaningful share of our conversations are with teams a year out from buying anything, and those tend to be the most useful ones for both sides. Tell us where you are and we'll match the conversation to it rather than pushing a process.
